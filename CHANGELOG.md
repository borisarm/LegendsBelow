# Changelog

All notable changes to LegendsBelow. Dates are commit dates.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Balance (a playtest-style pass, checked by a bot):
  - **`BalanceTests`**: a headless bot plays each class from floor 1 through the first boss to the resting place (it fights what is awake, casts what is ready, drinks potions when low, shoots and backs away with a bow or wand, then clears room after room) and reports deaths, potions, level, and minutes. Built only in optimized configurations (seconds in Release, minutes in Debug), so the Release CI job runs it.
  - Experience about halved (minion 12, skeleton warrior 30, crossbowman 22, skeleton mage 27, golem 60, Necromancer 200): the bot reached level 12-14 by the first boss, now about level 8 (with deaths, which respawn rooms).
  - Skeletons resist piercing by a quarter, not a half (daggers and arrows were badly behind); the rusted dagger does 1d4+1.
  - The Rogue starts with 11 hit points (was 8, fewer than the Mage), the Warrior with 6 stamina; Arcane Bolt and Frost Bolt cost 6 mana (were 10 and 8), so a new Mage casts twice; every class starts with 2 potions.
  - Fewer enemies on floors 1-2: combat rooms hold 2 light enemies until floor 3, elite rooms bring their caster and golem from floor 3.
- Deeper AI and bosses (design-combat-system.md 9):
  - **Group tactics**: fighters do not pass their group's tank (`enemies.json` `tank`: the skeleton warrior and the golem) until it is within 2.5 tiles of the player, and shooters keep two tiles behind the front line (though never out of their own range). `Formation`, `EnemyBehavior::RankGap`, `EngageDistance`.
  - **Telegraphed attacks** (`slamRadius`, `slamWindupSeconds`, `slamCooldownSeconds`, `slamTriggerRange`, `slamAtTarget`, `slamDamage`, `slamDamageType`; `EnemyAction::Slam`, `Enemy::slamSeconds`/`slamCenter`): a ring of red sparks on the ground, with a second ring growing to meet it, while the boss winds up; everyone inside when it lands is struck. The Necromancer raises an eruption under the player; the new Golem Warlord slams around itself.
  - **A boss for each tier** (`GameEngine::BossEnemies`, `BossForDepth`): the Necromancer ends floors 1-3, the **Golem Warlord** (a huge golem with a great axe, two phases, `loot_golem_warlord`) floors 4-6, then they take turns.
- Character growth (design-character-system.md 3):
  - **The Warrior's abilities**, paid in stamina (its mana pool, 6 at level 1, shown as `ST`; spell power from STR): Cleave (a sweep at everyone around), Shield Bash at level 3 (stuns the target, `status_stun_basic`), Whirlwind at level 5.
  - **The Rogue's**: Fan of Knives at level 3, Smoke Bomb at level 5 (blinds everyone around, `status_blinded_basic`, so every blow is a sneak attack).
  - A level up names the abilities it opens ("NEW: Shield Bash").
  - **Paths** at level 5 (`classes.json` `specializations`, `Content::SpecializationDefinition`, `ClassDefinition::SpecializationLevel`): Berserker or Guardian, Pyromancer or Archmage, Assassin or Shadow, Inquisitor or Guardian, each adding to damage, to-hit, armour class, armour, spell power or mana regeneration. Chosen once on the character screen (`ScreenAction::ChoosePath`; the HUD says "A PATH TO CHOOSE (I)"), kept in saves (`player.path`). `GameEngine::GetPlayerPath`, `ChoosePath`, `GrantExperience` (tools and tests).
- Dungeon variety (design-dungeon-and-world.md 7.1), the `:Decor` partition (`FloorDecor`, `FloorTheme`, `Prop`, `SpikeTrap`):
  - **A look per tier** (three floors each, `ThemeForDepth`): the crypts (floors 1-3, the Dungeon Pack 1.0 bricks), the caves (4-6: dirt floors, cracked mossy walls), the ruins (7-9: worn hexagonal tiles with weeds, walls broken open), then around again. KayKit Dungeon Pack 1.1 pieces (`dungeon_texture.png`).
  - **Props** in the rooms' corners, by room type and tier (barrels, crates, a table in the entrance, trunks and coins in treasure rooms, rocks and rubble in the caves), never by a doorway, the same for the same floor. They are solid: `WorldMap::AddObstacle` (round obstacles bodies slide around; sight passes over them), `ClearObstacles`, `ObstacleCount`.
  - **Spike traps** in the corridors from depth 2 (none on resting floors; more deeper): a grate whose spikes stir, then rise for a moment every 3 s (`GameSound::Spikes`), striking the player on the cell once a cycle for 1d6 + half the depth, piercing.
  - `GameEngine::GetDecor`, `GetTheme`, `EnterFloorPosition` (tools and tests).
- Resting places and the merchant (design-dungeon-and-world.md 7.3): the floor after each boss (depth 4, 7, 10: `GameEngine::IsRestDepth`) has no enemies ("DEPTH 4 - RESTING PLACE"). In its entrance stand a **shrine** (a decorated pillar and lit candles) that restores health and mana and lifts every status once ("BLESSED"), and a **merchant** (the KayKit Adventurers engineer). F or d-pad up by the merchant opens its screen (`InteractAction`, `GetTradeScreen`): potions, arrows and bolts in bundles of 20 without end, and six pieces of gear rolled for the depth (loot.json `stock_merchant`, with rarity); the bag sells at a quarter of the price. Items gain a `value` (weapons, armour, consumables) and rarities a `priceMultiplier`. `PriceOf`, `SellPriceOf`, `BuyFromMerchant`, `SellToMerchant`, `GetMerchantStock`, `GetMerchantPosition`, `GetShrinePosition`, `IsRestFloor`, `EnterFloor` (tools and tests). `ScreenAction::Buy`, `Sell`.
- Loot with rarity and affixes, item comparison, and ammunition (design-loot-and-progression.md 3-4):
  - **Rarity** (Assets/Data/rarities.json, `RarityDefinition`): dropped weapons and armour roll Common (white), Uncommon (green, 1 affix), Rare (blue, 2), or Epic (purple, 3); the odds shift with depth (`weightPerDepth`) from the design's early 70/20/8/2 to its late ~40/30/20/8 by depth 10 (the wave in the arena).
  - **Affixes** (Assets/Data/affixes.json, `AffixDefinition`): prefixes and suffixes for weapons and/or armour (Keen, Brutal, Vicious, Stalwart, Warding, Runed, of the Bear, of Precision, of the Turtle, of Focus, of the Sage) add damage, attack bonus, armour class, armour, spell power, or mana regeneration while equipped (`ItemBonuses`, `GameEngine::EquippedBonuses`). A rolled item is named from them ("Keen Worn Shortbow of Precision").
  - **Rolled items** (`ItemKind::Rolled`, `RolledItem`, `ItemRolls`, `LootRoller::RollRarity`, `RollAffixes`): each is its own bag entry; equipping, unequipping, picking up, and saves (`~base|rarity|affix,affix`) keep them. `GiveItemRef`, `EquipItemRef`, `ItemName`, `GetItemRolls`, `GetPlayerWeaponItem`.
  - **The character screen** colors items by rarity and compares each piece with what is equipped in its place: a weapon's average blow and an armour piece's armour, as a +/- difference.
  - **Ammunition**: bows shoot arrows and crossbows bolts (`consumable_ammo_arrow`, `consumable_ammo_bolt`: consumables gain `ammoType`, models are the Adventurers bundles); a shot spends one, and without any "NO ARROWS" floats instead of a shot. The HUD counts them while a bow or crossbow is in hand. The Warrior starts with 30 arrows, the Rogue with 40 (classes.json `startingItems` take "key:count"); minions, crossbowmen, and chests drop handfuls (loot.json items gain a `quantity` dice). Wands need none. `AmmoCount`.
- The Rogue and the Cleric are playable (all four classes on the "Choose your hero" page: `MenuCommand::PlayRogue`, `PlayCleric`):
  - **The Rogue** (the KayKit Adventurers hooded rogue, `Rogue_Hooded.glb`): a dagger, a shortbow in the bag, and two abilities on the bar (Utility spells, data in spells.json): **Evasion** rolls 3 tiles and no attack touches the rogue for half a second ("DODGE"), **Shadowstep** steps behind an enemy in range. **Sneak Attack** (design-character-system.md 3.2.2; classes.json `sneakAttackDice`, 2d6) adds dice to blows and shots against an enemy that has not noticed the rogue, is struck from behind (`GameEngine::FlankCosine`), cannot act or move, or within 2 s of a shadowstep (spent on a hit): "SNEAK ATTACK" floats over it. `WeaponAttackProfile::extraDice`, `WeaponAttackResult::extraDamage`.
  - **The Cleric** (the knight in its gold alternative texture: `Cleric.gltf`, `knight_texture_alt_B.png`): a mace, a wooden shield in the bag, and Smite (an instant strike), Divine Light (heals), Minor Cure (heals and lifts a hindrance: `cleansesStatuses`), and Turn Undead (level 5: undead around the target are held helpless).
  - New spell kinds (`SpellDefinition::IsTargetedStrike`, `IsTargetedBurst`, `IsHeal`, `IsDash`, `IsShadowstep`, `NeedsTarget`; spells.json `dashTiles`, `evadeSeconds`, `teleportBehindTarget`, `cleansesStatuses`); heals are base + coefficient x spell power (WIS for the Cleric; design-spells-system.md 5.2). A spell that needs a target says "NO TARGET" without one. `Encounter::StrikeWithSpell`, `StatusSet::RemoveHindrances`. The Rig_Medium MovementAdvanced set is loaded for the rogue's roll.
- The tactical pause and orders (design-combat-system.md 2.1):
  - Tab (gamepad left stick click) pauses the fight and resumes it (`TacticalPauseAction`, `IsTacticallyPaused`); the screen dims and a line explains the controls. Nothing simulates while paused; orders given meanwhile are carried out when it ends.
  - **Orders** (`GameEngine::PlayerOrder`, `OrderKind`, `GetPlayerOrder`): right click (the secondary action) on the floor walks there; on an enemy it goes after it and attacks until it falls (from range with a bow, crossbow, or wand, in sight). While paused, the arrows or d-pad pick an enemy (nearest first: `GetPickedEnemy`), Attack orders an attack on it, and a spell key orders that spell cast at it once in range. Moving, attacking, or casting yourself replaces an order.
  - Rings of sparks mark the picked enemy (gold), the order's target (red), and where a move order goes (green); the order in hand is named at the bottom of the screen.
  - `RenderingSystem::ScreenToFloor` and `IsometricCamera::ScreenToFloor` find the floor point under a pixel (the mouse).
- Bosses and brutes (design-dungeon-and-world.md 7.1-7.2, design-combat-system.md 9.3, game-design.md Elite & Boss Rules):
  - **The Necromancer** (`enemy_necromancer`, `loot_necromancer`): a boss guarding the exit room at the end of each tier of floors (depth 3, 6, 9: `GameEngine::BossFloorInterval`; the way down opens when its room is clear). It casts Shadow Grasp (slows; `projectileSpeed` added), keeps its distance, and raises skeleton minions while fighting; below half health it turns to a second phase, raising and attacking twice as often (a bell tolls). What it raised crumbles when it falls. Its health bar and name run across the top of the screen ("ENRAGED" in its second phase: `HudModel::Boss`).
  - **The Skeleton Golem** (`enemy_skeleton_golem`, `loot_skeleton_golem`): a slow brute on KayKit's Rig_Large with a great axe that hits hard and does not reel from hits. Elite rooms from depth 2, combat rooms from depth 5, arena waves from the fourth (`Encounter::WaveBrutes`; `Configure` takes it).
  - Enemies gain `rig` (Medium or Large), `staggerImmune`, `boss`, `summon`, `summonCount`, `maxSummons`, `summonCooldownSeconds`, and `phaseTwoHealthFraction`; summons must be known enemies that do not summon. `Enemy::wave`, `group`, `summoner`, `phaseTwo`; `Encounter::GetPhaseChanges`; `Spawn` takes a group.
  - **Groups**: a room's enemies (and a wave) notice the player together, also those out of sight.
  - The large rig's animation sets load with their clips named under "Large/" (`RenderingSystem::LoadAnimations` takes a name prefix; `GameEngine::LargeAnimationFiles`, `LargeClipPrefix`; `EnemyClips::large`).
- Spells and the Mage (design-spells-system.md 4-5, design-character-system.md 4.2 and 6):
  - **Class choice**: New Game opens a "Choose your hero" page (`MenuPage::ChooseClass`, `MenuCommand::PlayWarrior`, `PlayMage`); `EngineOptions::playerClass` and `GameEngine::GetPlayerClass`. Saves record the class (`player.class`); loading a save of another class starts a run as that class first.
  - **The Mage**: the KayKit Adventurers mage (`Mage.glb`) holding a wand; the class's kit comes from classes.json (`startingWeapon`, `startingItems`: the Warrior's sword and bow, the Mage's wand and staff). Wands shoot (they gain `range` and `projectileSpeed`): an orb of their damage type that lights the room.
  - **Mana and the spell bar**: classes.json gains `spellBar` (up to 4 castable spells), `manaRegenPerSecond`, and `spellPowerBase`. Keys 1-4 (gamepad LB, RB, Y, B: `GameEngine::SpellActions`) cast; a cast takes the spell's cast time with the player standing still, moving cancels it for free, and the mana is spent as the spell leaves; cooldowns run per slot. `GetSpellBar`, `SpellBlocker` ("level 5", "mana", "cooldown"), `GetSpellCooldown`, `IsPlayerCasting`, `GetPlayerSpellPower` (SP_Base + INT modifier x level for the Mage).
  - **Spells**: spells.json gains `projectileSpeed`; a spell is a missile (speed and range; with a radius it bursts where it lands) or a burst around the caster (a radius, no range): `SpellDefinition::IsMissile`, `IsBurstAroundCaster`, `IsCastable`. Damage is base + coefficient x spell power (`Encounter::SpellDamage`) through the target's defenses with no attack roll (`CombatService::ResolveSpellHit`). `Encounter::CastMissile`, `CastBurst`, `GetBursts`; projectiles carry a `SpellPayload`, and `CombatEvent::spell` names the spell of a hit. The Mage's bar: Arcane Bolt, Frost Bolt (slows), Fireball (level 5, bursts and burns), Frost Nova (level 5, roots everyone around).
  - **Status effects** (`:Statuses`, `StatusSet`, Assets/Data/statuses.json, `StatusDefinition`, `ContentDatabase::FindStatus`): slowed, rooted, frozen, burning, shocked, and charmed, from spells' `appliedStatusIds` (checked against statuses.json). They slow or stop enemies and the player, prevent actions, or deal damage every tick; slowed characters are tinted icy, burning ones smoulder, and the HUD names the player's.
  - **Skeleton Mage** (`enemy_skeleton_mage`, `loot_skeleton_mage`): casts Frost Bolt from range and backs away while recovering. Enemies gain `spell` and `spellPower` (`EnemyDefinition::IsCaster`). Casters join the arena's waves from the third (`Encounter::WaveCasters`) and the dungeon's combat rooms from depth 3 (elite rooms from depth 2).
  - **Look and sound**: spell missiles are glowing orbs with a point light and a trail in their element's colors (`ElementPalette`; `ParticleEffect::SpellTrail`, `SpellBurst`, `SpellCharge`; `EffectsSystem::Emit` takes an `EffectPalette`); magic gathers in the caster's hands; bursts flare. The HUD shows a mana bar, the spell bar (key, name, cost, cooldown, why a spell is blocked), and a cast bar; the character screen lists the spells with their costs and damage. Sounds: `GameSound::SpellCast`, `SpellHit`, `FireBurst`, `FrostBurst`, `NoMana` (Kenney glass and soft impacts).
- Ranged combat (design-combat-system.md 7.4 line of sight, 9.1 Kite):
  - **Projectiles** (`:Projectiles`, `ProjectileSet`, `Projectile`, `ProjectileSide`, `ProjectileTarget`, `ProjectileImpact`): arrows and bolts fly at chest height along their heading, swept in 0.1-tile steps so fast ones cannot pass through a body, hit the first body of the other side, and stick in walls for 1.5 s (fading out). They carry the shooter's attack and are resolved when they arrive, so they can be dodged. `Encounter` owns them: `Shoot` (the player's), `GetProjectiles`, `GetShots` and `GetWallHits` (this step's enemy shots and wall strikes); `Update` now also returns the player's projectile hits (`CombatEventKind::PlayerAttack`).
  - **Skeleton Crossbowman** (`enemy_skeleton_rogue`: KayKit Skeleton Rogue with the skeletons' crossbow and arrow, `loot_skeleton_rogue`): shoots only at a player in sight, from up to 7 tiles; while reloading it backs away from a player closer than 3.5 tiles unless a wall is behind it. `EnemyBehavior::Update` returns an `EnemyAction` (`None`, `Strike`, `Shoot`). Crossbowmen join the arena's waves from the second (`Encounter::WaveShooters`; `Configure` takes the ranged type) and the dungeon's combat rooms from depth 2 (and every elite room).
  - **Content**: enemies gain `projectileSpeed`, `projectileModel`, and `keepAwayRange` (`EnemyDefinition::IsRanged`); weapons gain `range` and `projectileSpeed` (`WeaponDefinition::IsRanged`), required for bows and crossbows and refused for anything else. The Worn Shortbow shoots 8 tiles, the Light Crossbow 9. Shortbows and crossbows drop from minions, crossbowmen, and chests.
  - **The player's bows and crossbows**: Attack draws and looses a shot at the nearest enemy in range and in sight (straight ahead without one), leaving at `GameEngine::ShotReleaseFraction` of the attack time; the player stands still until then. The starting bow (`GameEngine::StartingBow`) is in the bag from the start. `GameEngine::GetPlayerWeapon` is public.
  - Sounds: `GameSound::Shoot` (cloth whip) for every shot, `GameSound::ArrowHitsWall` (wood knock) where one strikes stone, with a puff of dust there; crossbowmen aim quietly.
- The player is the animated KayKit Adventurers knight (`Knight.glb`, at the skeletons' scale on the same Rig_Medium) instead of the rigid Dungeon Pack knight: it idles, runs, slashes (one-handed, two-handed, or stabs with a dagger), draws and releases a bow or shoots a crossbow (the CombatRanged animation set is loaded), flinches when hit, and falls. The equipped weapon is drawn in its hand slot (KayKit Adventurers sword, axe, dagger, staff, bow, and crossbow; the skeletons' mace), a worn shield on its left arm, and crossbows turned to a pistol grip (`GameEngine::PistolGripModels`).

### Changed
- Divine Light costs 10 mana (from 12: a new cleric has 11).
- The Mage starts with 10 hit points (classes.json level 1 `hpGain` 6 -> 10): with 6, two skeleton minions brought a new mage down in seconds.

### Build
- MSVC static analysis: `-p:LBStaticAnalysis=true` (`Scripts/Test.ps1 -StaticAnalysis`) compiles with `/analyze` and fails on findings; it is a fourth CI job. Fixed what it found: root-parameter and sampler arrays filled in element by element are value-initialized, and `CharacterService`'s class-rules lookups check the rules separately before taking a reference. GoogleTest's macros trip C6326 and C6262, which are off in the test projects only.
- The engine tests are split by area into eleven files (from one 3,650-line `test.cpp`), with their shared setup and helpers in `EngineTestHelpers.h`; the same 227 tests.
- vcpkg runs in manifest mode only (`Directory.Build.props` turns off its classic mode): the projects outside the manifest no longer get the machine's global `<vcpkg root>\installed` headers on their include path, so a dependency missing from `vcpkg.json` fails everywhere instead of building only where it happens to be installed.
- Line endings pinned in `.gitattributes`: text is stored with LF and checked out with CRLF on every machine, whatever `core.autocrlf` says; binary assets (`.glb`, `.bin`, `.png`, `.ico`, `.ttf`) are never converted and the UTF-16 `resource.rc` is left alone.
- vcpkg package versions are pinned: `vcpkg-configuration.json` names the microsoft/vcpkg registry at a fixed baseline (the commit the local builds used), so CI and every machine resolve the same versions.
- Module code no longer relies on names `import std` does not guarantee: fixed-width integers and `size_t` are qualified as `std::` (545 uses) and the 20 `using namespace std;` directives in module interfaces are gone.
- Every project compiles at `/W4` with warnings as errors (`Directory.Build.targets`; headers included with angle brackets, the vcpkg and GoogleTest code, are treated as external and compiled without warnings). Fixed what `/W4` found: unused parameters of placeholder service APIs, a shadowed variable in `RenderingSystem.cpp`, an unused test variable, and signed/unsigned comparisons in tests.
- AddressSanitizer builds: `-p:LBAddressSanitizer=true` instruments every project (turning off the runtime checks and the STL container annotations it cannot be combined with here). The whole test suite runs clean under it.
- `Scripts/Test.ps1` builds and runs every test executable (`-Configuration`, `-AddressSanitizer`, `-NoBuild`), failing on a build error, a failed test, an ASan report, or a test executable that did not run.
- Continuous integration (`.github/workflows/build.yml`): Debug, Release, and Debug with AddressSanitizer build and test on every push to master and pull request, on GitHub's Windows Server 2025 image (Visual Studio 2026), with vcpkg's binary cache kept between runs.

### Fixed
- Decoded images (`GltfLoader.cpp`) and glyph distance fields (`FontLoader.cpp`) are held in `std::unique_ptr` with their library's free function, so they are released even if copying them out throws.
- `wWinMain` pairs its setup with scope guards: COM is uninitialized (it never was), and the game runtime is shut down on every exit path, not only the two it was before.
- The player's character is looked up through one checked `GameEngine::PlayerCharacter()` in the bag, equipment, character screen, and save code, instead of dereferencing the lookup unchecked.
- `GameEngine::Shutdown` followed by `Initialize` (or a second `Initialize`, or one that failed part way) no longer carries state from the previous run into the next. A dungeon engine re-initialized as the arena kept the old floor's torches, whose wall indices pointed past the new walls (out-of-bounds reads on the next frame), along with the old particles, explored map cells, and map toggle. All per-run state now lives in `GameEngine::Session` (and loaded model handles in `GameEngine::Assets`); `Shutdown` replaces both wholesale, also after a failed `Initialize`, and `Initialize` starts with `Shutdown`.

### Changed
- `Encounter` split by responsibility into `LB.Engine` partitions: `:Enemies` (the enemy data types: `Enemy`, `EnemyState`, `EnemyClips`, `CombatEvent`, `PlayerTarget`), `:EnemyBehavior` (`EnemyBehavior`: one enemy's state machine and animations, chasing around walls, turning, attacking, keeping bodies apart; it reports a landed strike and the encounter resolves it), and `:Waves` (`Waves`: wave composition and scaling, spawn points). `Encounter` keeps the roster, the wave timer, spawning, and resolving attacks both ways; its public names are unchanged (aliases of the parts'). `Distance` and `Forward` are free functions of `:World`. Behavior and the dice sequence are unchanged.
- `CombatService`: the methods that roll dice (`RollDice`, `RollAttack`, `ResolveWeaponAttack`, `SpellAttackHits`, `MakesSavingThrow`) are no longer `const`, and the random generator is no longer `mutable`; the pure calculations stay `const`.
- `GameEngine` keeps its run state (`Session`, about 20 KB) on the heap: `Shutdown` swaps in a new one instead of building a large temporary on the stack.
- `LB.Core`'s parts are its partitions: `LB.Common`, `LB.CoreValueObjects`, and `LB.Entity` became `LB.Core:Common`, `:ValueObjects`, and `:Entity` (files `Common.ixx`, `ValueObjects.ixx`, `Entity.ixx`), importing each other as siblings. `import LB.Core` is the only way in; nothing could import `LB.Common` by mistake anymore.
- `LB::Entity` is a plain value base: no virtual destructor (nothing deletes entities through it; every entity carried a vtable pointer for nothing) and no hand-written `operator!=` (C++20 derives it).
- The particle pool (`ParticleField`) is a deque: at the 4,000 cap the oldest particle is dropped in constant time instead of shifting the whole pool.
- `GameEngine` split by responsibility. Self-contained parts became their own `LB.Engine` partitions, each owning its state and testable without an engine: `:Walls` (`WallSet`: placing wall pieces, fading those that hide the player), `:Torches` (`TorchSet`: placement, flicker, lights, fire), `:Minimap` (`Minimap`, `MinimapMarkers`), `:Effects` (`EffectsSystem`: the particle pool, the effect recipes including torch flames and embers, drawing), `:CombatText` (`CombatTextLog`, `FloatingText`), and `:Hud` (`Hud::Draw` from a `HudModel` snapshot). The remaining method bodies moved out of `GameEngine.ixx` (about 1,930 lines, now about 750) into implementation units by concern: `EngineSetup.cpp` (startup, shutdown, saves), `Simulation.cpp` (one step), `WorldView.cpp` (drawing). The public API is unchanged (constants such as `TorchLightRadius` and `FadedWallAlpha` are aliases of the parts' own).
- Model lookups by file go through one `GameEngine::ModelOf` with transparent hashing (no `std::string` built per lookup), replacing four copies of the same lambda.
- The renderer's `GpuRenderer` (one 1,100-line class in `DeviceResources.cpp`) split into internal partitions, one job each: `:DeviceResources` (the GDK device and swap chain template only), `:GpuResources` (upload memory, the texture heap and uploads, built-in textures, one-off command lists), `:ModelPipeline` (GPU models, frame constants and lights, the skin palette, instanced draws), `:PrimitivePipeline` (DirectXTK12 BasicEffect and unit shapes), `:OverlayPipeline`, `:ParticlePipeline`, and `:GpuRenderer` composing them (frame lifecycle, camera, device loss). The Win32/D3D12 include list is shared in `Direct3D.h`. Mapped upload buffers are unmapped by a scope guard. Nothing outside the module changes.

### Added
- Framed menus (Kenney Fantasy UI Borders, CC0, in `Assets/UI`): the menu panels and the character screen get a gold double frame with notched corners, the selected item a framed highlight, and page headings (and the title's subtitle) ornaments either side. `MenuArt`, `MenuView::Panel`/`Selection`/`HeadingOrnaments`, `EngineOptions::uiDirectory`; without it the menus are plain panels. Menu panels are sized to their content, and the HUD hides under the character screen too.

Images are uploaded beside the font atlases (and again after a device loss) and ordered with rectangles and text by layer; `GetQueuedImages` for tests.
- The HUD, menus, character screen, minimap, and floating texts scale with the window: the 2D layer is laid out in UI units for the default 800 x 600 window and drawn at `GameEngine::UiScaleFor(width, height)` (as large as fits both ways, so it keeps its proportions; at least half size). `RenderingSystem::SetUiScale`/`GetUiScale`, `GetUiSize` (the window in UI units), and `WorldToUi` (a world point in UI units); the overlay scales every rectangle, glyph, and outline together.
- Menus (`:Menus`: `GameMenu`, `MenuView`; `GameMenus.cpp`). A title screen at startup over the dimmed dungeon: New Game, Continue (the quick save), Options, Credits, Quit (`EngineOptions::titleScreen`, on in the game; `GameEngine::IsQuitRequested` asks the host to close). Esc or Start opens the pause menu (the game pauses while any menu is open; the music plays on): Resume, Save Game, Load Game, Options, Credits, Quit to Title (a fresh run behind the title). Credits show `Assets/Data/credits.txt`, which now credits everything shipped, including the CC-BY attribution "Mystical Theme" requires. Up/down, left/right (A/D, arrows, D-pad), Enter/Space/A, and Esc/B navigate; disabled items are skipped; the HUD hides while a menu is open. A new run starts with `GameEngine::StartRun` (also the end of `Initialize`).
- Music (`Assets/Music`, credits in `Assets/Music/CREDITS.txt`): two layers loop together and crossfade with the fight: the calm theme (the dungeon's "mystical theme", or "Awesomeness" in the wave arena) while exploring, "Battle Theme A" from the moment an enemy is after the player until things have been quiet for 4 seconds. ### Changed
- Content loading needs `enemies.json`, `consumables.json`, and `loot.json` next to the other data files; enemies name a loot table (`lootTable`), checked against loot.json, whose items are checked against the item files.
- Overlay rectangles are drawn as alpha-blended quads instead of render-target clears; the overlay (rectangles, text, sprites) counts as one draw call.
- `WorldPoint` moved from `LB.Engine:GameEngine` to `LB.Engine:World` (still exported by `LB.Engine`). `Encounter::Configure` takes the `WorldMap` instead of the arena size.
- The model shaders take the material color and emissive per part as root constants and sample a base-color texture (a white texel for untextured materials); the instance stream carries the copy's tint and its skin palette offset.
- `CharacterService` uses data-driven class rules: HP/MP come from the class progression tables (MaxHP = table HP + CON_mod x level; MaxMP = table MP + INT/WIS_mod x level for Mages/Clerics), replacing the hard-coded formulas. Characters can only be created for classes whose rules are configured; the engine configures them from classes.json and runs without a player when there is no content.
- Removed the hard-coded class attribute bonus on creation (classes.json starting attributes are the class template) and the fixed per-level attribute gains. Armour proficiency lists replaced by the armour adjustment model; armour.json `allowedClasses` is no longer cross-checked against them.
- The render target is sRGB (shading in linear space, matching glTF colors); the engine converts its display-space colors, and the default lighting is dimmed with almost no specular.
- fastgltf and simdjson are static (overlay triplets) and built with `/guard:cf /guard:ehcont`; LBRenderingSystem drops `/GL` in Release (corrupt module debug info, LNK4020).
- Movement is screen-relative through the camera: W/stick up walks away from the camera whatever its rotation. The background is a dim ambient color instead of cornflower blue.
- `LB.Engine` re-exports `LB.RenderingSystem`. DirectXTK12 added to `vcpkg.json` and linked statically into `LBRenderingSystem.lib` through overlay triplets (`triplets/`, `vcpkg-configuration.json`) that keep every other port dynamic; no DirectXTK12.dll ships.

- `vcpkg.json` moved to the repository root so all projects share one manifest.
- LBEngineCore references only the projects it imports (LBRenderingSystem, LBModel.Core).
