## 10. Class-Oriented Weapon Themes

### 10.1 Warrior

- Prefers swords, axes, maces, polearms, two-handed weapons.
- Signature examples:
  - Vanguard Sabre
  - Templar Greatblade
  - Berserker’s Cleaver

### 10.2 Rogue

- Prefers daggers, short swords, bows, crossbows.
- Signature examples:
  - Assassin’s Knife
  - Nightstalker’s Fang
  - Shadowbow

### 10.3 Mage

- Prefers staves, wands, and spell foci.
- Signature examples:
  - Archmage Staff
  - Ember/Frost/Storm Wands

### 10.4 Cleric

- Prefers maces, hammers, staves.
- Signature examples:
  - Templar’s Mace
  - Inquisitor’s Hammer
  - Staff of the Below
